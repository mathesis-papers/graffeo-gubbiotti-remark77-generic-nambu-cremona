Graffeo–Gubbiotti, Remark 7.7 (arXiv:2301.09129v2, pp. 35–37): Nambu conjugacy and the twelve-plane configuration

Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Published paper · Version 2 · CC BY 4.0

PDFSourceCitationRepositoryMathesis recordLean source

Abstract

We prove the projective-conjugacy conjecture in Graffeo–Gubbiotti, Remark 7.7, arXiv:2301.09129v2, pp. 35–37, for quadratic Nambu Hamiltonians with a positive-definite first form and simple generalized spectrum. An explicit simultaneous-congruence reduction and Euler cubic identity give the conjugator. The six reducible net members split into the twelve planes v_i±v_j=0 through eight projective sign points, with six planes at every point and four points on every plane; the conjugator sends the points to the normal-form tetrahedra. The written proof extends conjugacy to regular complex symmetric pencils with three distinct projective determinant roots. For real positive-definite first form, repeated generalized eigenvalues give degree 2n+1 in the double case and degree 1 in the scalar case, complementing the cited generic law 2n^2+1.
